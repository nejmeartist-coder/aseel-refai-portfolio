About Me

I am a technical Instructional Designer and Learning Experience Specialist focused on building intuitive, software-driven employee onboarding frameworks. My design approach pairs instructional psychology models (ADDIE/SAM) with crisp visual layouts to reduce student cognitive load.

With an extensive background configuring platform settings and running user reporting tracks for over 500 active organization members, I design learning paths that prioritize data alignment, straightforward user provisioning, and measurable business outcomes.
